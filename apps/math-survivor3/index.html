HOW IT WORKS

  • Type the value of any sigil on the field into the console. Digits and −.

  • Wrong value? The blast fizzles. No harm but time lost.
  • WASD / arrows — reposition inside the ring. SHIFT — dash. SPACE — Arcane Nova (panic push, needs a full meter).
